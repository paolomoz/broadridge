Singapore, July 15, 2024 – To transform and expand their prime brokerage business lines and as part of its broader efforts to add and enhance value for its clientele, AmInvestment Bank, a company in the Ambank Group and one of the largest banking groups in Malaysia, has deployed global Fintech leader Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc.‘s (NYSE:BR), Securities Finance and Collateral Management platform to enable their Securities Borrowing and Lending product offerings. This single platform allows AmInvestment Bank to simplify and innovate trading, manage collateral, and handle trade lifecycle events while integrating seamlessly with their existing systems.
