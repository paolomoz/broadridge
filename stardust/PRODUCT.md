## Platform

web

## Users

Institutional financial-services decision-makers: capital-markets operations and technology leaders, wealth-management executives, asset managers, corporate issuers (IR / corporate secretary teams), and consumer-industries communications buyers. Secondary audiences: investors (NYSE: BR investor relations), job seekers (Careers), and existing clients (Client access portal).
_provenance: captured — nav "Who we serve" segments (Asset Management, Capital Markets, Issuers, Wealth Management, Consumer Industries) and footer audience links._

## Product Purpose

Broadridge is a global fintech infrastructure and communications provider ("Powering the pulse of the financial industry"). The site's job is enterprise lead generation and brand authority: route each audience segment to relevant capabilities (proxy/governance, trading and post-trade, customer communications, data & AI), prove credibility (60 years, 15,000 associates, $15T settled daily, 10,000 public companies served, industry awards), and convert through "Contact us" lead forms present on nearly every page.
_provenance: captured — home/about copy, stat carousels, cross-page contact-form band._

## Positioning

Scale-based infrastructure claims competitors cannot truthfully copy: processing over $15T in equity and fixed-income trades daily, serving 10,000+ public companies, running the dominant proxy/shareholder-communications rails for corporate governance. Recent positioning leans into tokenization, digital assets, and AI ("NAVIGATE tokenized markets with confidence", Tokenization Pulse Study, agentic AI insights).
_provenance: captured — hero carousel, stat proof points, insights rail._

## Operating Context

Buyers evaluate via long B2B cycles: research reports (Digital Transformation Study), analyst coverage (IDC, Celent), webinars, and direct sales contact. The site integrates: Vimeo (brand/solution videos), Qualified chat widget, Workday (careers), a gated-report lead-capture pattern, global offices/locale variants (/de, /ca, /jp, /cit sitemaps), and a client login portal.
_provenance: captured — embeds, forms, sitemap index, utility nav._

## Capabilities and Constraints

Site is a large corporate marketing property: 1,044 URLs in the authoritative us-en sitemap across ~10 content types (segment pages, capability pages, insights/articles, press releases ~345, resources, campaign pages, legal, hubs). Legacy IA (/financial-services/...) 301-redirects to current IA (/who-we-serve/, /capability/). Regulated-industry constraints: legal/privacy surface is extensive (Privacy Statement, Do Not Sell, accessibility statement, cookie consent).
_provenance: captured — sitemaps, robots.txt, redirects, footer legal links._

## Product Principles

1. Segment-first routing — every journey starts from "who you are" (Who we serve) or "what you need" (Capabilities). _inferred from nav architecture._
2. Authority through proof — stats, awards, and analyst recognition carry the persuasion load, not adjectives. _inferred from repeated stat/award bands._
3. Every page converts — the contact-form band ("What's next for your business?") closes nearly every template. _captured, 4/5 pages._
4. Insight as fuel — a large, continuously-published insights/press engine feeds every commercial page via cross-promo rails. _captured._
