Retirement Product Evaluator

Research, evaluate, and compare different retirement plan products with a single, flexible platform that lets advisory firms and individual advisors alike, streamline the due diligence process when evaluating retirement products and crafting customized plan recommendations.

Schedule a demo

One evaluation tool for all retirement products

  • Compare a wide range of products using hard to find data within a standardized due diligence framework
  • Evaluate products against advisor and plan requirements to identify those that meet defined criteria
  • Apply weighted scoring to determine which product best aligns with the objectives of the plan
  • Generate documentation for the rationale behind your recommendations
  • Track products to support a consistent, fiduciary monitoring process
  • Capture data that is fragmented or hard to obtain from manufacturers

A flexible platform, configurable to your firm’s unique needs

Retirement Product Evaluator is flexible, offering a single platform and user experience across modules.

Assess available target date series across 50+ data points, such as expenses, investment philosophy, and glidepath. This independent database is sourced directly from target date series managers, to help you evaluate multiple series side-by-side and eliminate manual collection of target date series data.

Evaluate stable value products across 80+ data points, including crediting rates, expenses, sector allocation, duration, and more. Data is collected from fund manufacturers, covering CITs, general accounts, and separate accounts, as well as 90+ funds and 325+ share classes.

Review in-plan retirement income products across 60+ factors, such as product type, income guarantee, accessibility, longevity protection, portability, fee structure, and more. Data is collected in partnership with CANNEX and includes guaranteed lifetime withdrawal benefit (GLWB) and guaranteed minimum withdrawal benefit (GMWB) from 15+ manufacturers.

Compare managed account programs on 60+ factors, including investments, advice and wellness, plan-level reporting, participant level data, fees, and more. Data is collected directly from some of the largest and most well-known providers in the industry.

How does Retirement Product Evaluator compare to other evaluation tools?

Retirement Product Evaluator is focused on deep, product-level comparisons of target date series, stable value funds, retirement income products, and managed account programs. It is designed to help advisors and firms enhance due diligence efficiency and fiduciary support with ready access to unbiased data.

Other fiduciary tools address broader plan-level governance, investment policy support, and monitoring, but do not offer the modular product-level research capabilities of Retirement Product Evaluator.

Where does the data come from in Retirement Product Evaluator?

Broadridge collects data gathered directly from product manufacturers and complements it with information from specialized third-party partners (like CANNEX) to build the database for Retirement Product Evaluator. The data is regularly updated and reviewed to provide users access to timely and unbiased data across all four product types.

Coverage includes 90+ stable value funds and 325+ share classes, 15+ retirement income product manufacturers, an expanding set of managed account programs, and multiple target date series sourced directly from fund managers.

How frequently is data in Retirement Product Evaluator updated?

Data is updated on a regular schedule, with frequency calibrated to each product type and data source.

Where can I get more information about the tool and coverage?

Your Broadridge representative can provide you additional information about the tool and current coverage details, as well as help you evaluate which modules or other Broadridge fiduciary tools may be suitable for your needs.
