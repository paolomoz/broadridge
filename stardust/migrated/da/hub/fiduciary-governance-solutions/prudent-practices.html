What are the Prudent Practices ® ?

The Prudent Practices ® are a globally recognized set of fiduciary best practices that help investment professionals meet their legal and ethical responsibilities. They define clear steps for managing investments with care, loyalty, and transparency while ensuring fiduciary integrity and accountability.

How do the Prudent Practices ® relate to Broadridge’s fiduciary training and tools?

The Prudent Practices ® form the basis of Broadridge’s fiduciary training programs, helping students learn and apply best practices for acting as investment fiduciaries. They also guide our fiduciary tools, including the Fiduciary Focus Toolkit ® , enabling advisors to efficiently implement fiduciary best practices for their clients.

How do the Prudent Practices ® support fiduciary excellence and compliance?

The Prudent Practices ® are organized under a four step Fiduciary Quality Management System (FQMS): Organize, Formalize, Implement, and Monitor. This structure builds consistency and legal defensibility, helping fiduciaries show procedural prudence and meet a recognized fiduciary standard of care.

Who are the Prudent Practices ® Handbooks designed for?

The Prudent Practices ® Handbooks are written for experienced fiduciaries, including Advisors, Managers, and Stewards. Each handbook offers tailored guidance for those roles, helping professionals apply fiduciary principles consistently and maintain compliance with established standards.
