Broadridge Awarded Best Global Proxy Voting Service Award at Goodacre’s Systems in The City Awards

LONDON, July 14, 2023 — Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc. (NYSE:BR), a global Fintech leader, has been awarded the Systems in The City Award for Best Global Proxy Voting Service at this year’s ceremony. The awards are hosted by the financial services consultancy group Goodacre, a renowned service and technology supplier to the regulated financial community in the UK, and celebrate the leading systems and services in the regulated financial services sector. The award recognises Broadridge’s commitment to driving innovation focused on transforming the proxy voting ecosystem for both institutional and retail investors.

“Congratulations to the team at Broadridge for taking home the Best Global Proxy Voting Service award for their continued success in leveraging technology to transform corporate governance globally and empowering investor stewardship,” said Stephen Pinner, Managing Director at Goodacre UK. “Broadridge has a proven and innovative track record which supports efficiency and greater levels of shareholder engagement. This award is a testament to their commitment.”

Broadridge’s established market utility for Global Proxy and corporate governance continues to evolve as a result of substantial investments in people, technology, infrastructure and through the establishment of a digitally connected worldwide network of key local market infrastructure providers.

“We’re proud to have been awarded the Best Global Proxy Voting Service award at Goodacre’s Systems in the City Awards for the fifth year running,” said Demi Derem, General Manager of International Investor Communication Solutions at Broadridge. “At Broadridge, we’re committed to driving overall industry change for the benefit of all participants in the investor communications lifecycle from issuers to investors, supporting the growing desire of investors globally who want to exercise their corporate governance entitlements. Our professional integrity, proven track record of innovation and best-in-class client service are all pivotal and key consideration points for our clients worldwide – and it is fantastic that this has been recognised once again.”
