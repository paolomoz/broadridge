Web hosting and Shareholder Email Enhancements

Broadridge is pleased to announce that we have modernized our Prospectus Express (PE) Web hosting Platform and Tailored Shareholder Report (TSR) email templates. We’ve streamlined the user interface, enhanced integration between email, web hosting, and improved the shareholder experience – delivering greater value to you.

Enhancements include:

  • Modernized look and feel with increased brand presence
  • A new “Investments Listed in Your Email” section that consolidates all fund document links from a TSR email directly on the landing page.
  • A Pass-Through Voting web hosting banner that makes it easier for shareholders to set voting preferences directly from Prospectus Express without returning to the email.

You can preview these changes starting today in UAT. Enhancements will be launched on May 15, 2026.

For any questions regarding these updates, please reach out to your client service representative.

FAQ

How do I access this new experience?

The experience is triggered when you click on a link (such as the annual report or prospectus link) directly from your TSR email. You will land on a newly designed Prospectus Express (PE) page that consolidates all relevant information from that specific email in one place.

Note: If you access Prospectus Express (PE)/Variable Products Express (VPX) through a company's website (outside of the email), you will not see the "Investments Listed in Your Email" section or the Pass-Through Voting banner. The standard experience will be displayed.

Who will see this new experience?

This new experience is available to shareholders who:

1. Receive a TSR email through the Shareholder Mail email delivery process

2. Click a link within that email that directs them to a page hosted by a company that uses the Prospectus Express (PE) web hosting platform

Will I need to do anything to see the new experience?

No, this will be the default experience on the go-live date.

What is the "Investments Listed in Your Email" (or "Your Information from Email") section?

This section appears on the left-hand side of the Prospectus Express (PE)/Variable Products Express (VPX) page and displays all the fund documents and links that were included in the specific email you received. The goal is to keep everything in one place so you don't have to toggle back and forth between the email and the Prospectus Express (PE)/Variable Products Express (VPX) page.

Why do I only see a few funds listed? I have more funds being offered.

The funds displayed are specific to the email you clicked from — not your entire portfolio. For example, if your email contained two Hextone funds, only those two funds will appear in this section.

How long will this information be available on the page?

The information will be available for approximately 18 months from the date the email was sent.

What if a shareholder receives multiple emails?

Each email has its own unique experience. Clicking a link in a second email will show only the funds and documents from that specific email. It does not erase or replace the previous email's experience each email link maintains its own view.

What happens if the "Investments Listed in Your Email" section doesn't appear?

If the system is unable to retrieve the email data, the "Investments Listed in Your Email" and Pass-Through Voting sections will simply not display . The shareholder will still see the standard Prospectus Express (PE)/Variable Products Express (VPX) page with the document links. No incorrect or misleading information will be shown.

What is the Pass-Through Voting banner?

If the shareholder email includes pass-through voting information, a banner will appear at the top of the Prospectus Express (PE) page to draw your attention to the pass-through voting program. This is designed to make it easier for the shareholder to set your voting policy preferences without having to go back to the email.

Will someone always see the Pass-Through Voting banner?

No. The banner will only appear if the specific email includes pass-through voting content. If pass-through voting is not part of the email, the banner will not be displayed.

Does this apply to all types of communications, or just TSR emails?

At launch, the "Investments Listed in Your Email" section is limited to TSR emails only. The updated look/feel for web hosting will apply to all document types (e.g., prospectus, summary prospectus).
