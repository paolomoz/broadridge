Global funds face unique challenges around regulatory reporting and compliance, especially when it comes to translating complex financial documentation. Our team of experienced translators, equipped with proprietary technology, are dedicated to the investment industry, span jurisdictions and are fluent in a global range of languages.

Expert services and innovative technology

We equip global translators dedicated to the investment industry with technologies that automate key processes to quickly and accurately translate the entire range of financial documentation.

Extensive Document Coverage

We primarily translate documents such as fund prospectus, supplements & addendums, annual & interim financial statements, application forms, shareholder communications, UCITS KIIDs, PRIIPs KIDs, factsheet and web content. This list is not exhaustive however and we can work with our clients on any fund related language task.

Targeted Experience Ensures Quality

We have 20 years of experience translating financial documentation for leading global investment managers for unparalleled accuracy and consistency. Our in-house translators are on-hand to meet fast turnaround times and tight deadlines.

Innovative Technology Adds Efficiency

Our translators rely on proprietary automation technology to add quality, efficiency and speed to their services. Our platform mutualises costs by leveraging previously translated text or text that is available in our investment industry glossaries and adheres to a proven quality control framework.
