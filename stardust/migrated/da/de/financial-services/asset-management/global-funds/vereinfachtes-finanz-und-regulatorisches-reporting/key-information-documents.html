Key Information Documents

Providing investors with critical information is not just good service. It’s a regulatory requirement. We understand the challenges of managing Key Information Documents and make the process easy, efficient and reliable — from composition to distribution and web-based access for investors.

End-to-End Management of Key Information Documents

We deliver managed services and hosted solutions for production, compliance, monitoring and distribution of UCITS KIIDs and PRIIPs KIDs.

UCITS KIID Services

Our comprehensive, end-to-end KIID outsourcing solution includes pro-active management, professional expertise, cost-effective processes and rigorous evidential control systems to support you from pre-production through to compliance monitoring.

PRIIPs KID Services

The PRIIPs KID is a new pre-contractual data rich document that applies to a wide range of products. Leveraging on our UCITS KIID expertise, we now offer purpose built data collation, automated production and dissemination of the PRIIPs KID and EPT/CEPT.

Full Calculation Solution

Our solution will calculate the figures which are required to be shown on the documents; for UCITS KIID the SRRI, OGC and Past Performance and for PRIIPs KID the SRI, Performance Scenarios, OOC, Transaction Costs including arrival price sourcing along with the Reduction in Yield (RIY). Each calculation performed by the system is accessible at all times on the client interface via a calculation audit file, allowing full transparency over the data within the calculation and the method used to reach the final value.
