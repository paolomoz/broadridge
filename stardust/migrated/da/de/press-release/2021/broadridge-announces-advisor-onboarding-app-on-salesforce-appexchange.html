Broadridge Announces Advisor Onboarding App on Salesforce AppExchange, the World's Leading Enterprise Cloud Marketplace

New York, NY, October 28, 2021– Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc. (NYSE:BR), a global Fintech leader, today announced it has launched the Advisor Onboarding app on Salesforce AppExchange, the World's Leading Enterprise Cloud Marketplace, empowering broker-dealers and wealth management firms to onboard advisors quickly and efficiently via a straight-through automated advisor onboarding process that covers the entire onboarding lifecycle. Advisor Onboarding supports advisor recruiting as well as M&A driven advisor onboarding activities.

Built on the Salesforce Platform, Advisor Onboarding is currently available on AppExchange.

“The Advisor Onboarding app enables firms to attract and onboard top advisor talent more swiftly in an increasingly competitive landscape, enabling firms to generate revenue more quickly,” said Mike Alexander, President of Wealth Management at Broadridge. “We are excited to build with a technology leader like Salesforce to continue to create and integrate innovative solutions that add to our core wealth offerings.”

Wealth Management firms looking to grow AUM by attracting top talent and delivering enhanced client experiences are challenged by the complexity of financial, legal, and operational workflow issues present throughout the advisor onboarding lifecycle. This solution brings together the distinct capabilities of Broadridge and Salesforce to automate onboarding processes and unlock data insights to reduce compliance risks, increase advisor and client satisfaction, and improve advisor and branch productivity; allowing firms and advisors to accelerate the time to first sale and generate income quickly and efficiently.

Advisor Onboarding

Broadridge’s Advisor Onboarding solution automates the advisor onboarding process and ongoing maintenance providing a guided wizard approach to collect pertinent data, built-in checklists for review and approval, population of regulatory and proprietary forms and integration with third party systems such as FINRA, fingerprinting, and background checks. This allows firms to onboard producing advisors faster, reduce risk exposure of undesirable advisors and support a positive experience for the new advisor.

Salesforce AppExchange enables wealth management firms to easily integrate Broadridge’s leading fintech solutions with the Salesforce platform.
