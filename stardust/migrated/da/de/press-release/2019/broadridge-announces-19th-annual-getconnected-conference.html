Broadridge Announces 19th Annual GetConnected Conference

auto

NEW YORK, N.Y. – MAY 14, 2019 – Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc. (NYSE:BR), a global Fintech leader and part of the S&P 500® Index, today announced the keynote speaker lineup for its 19th annual GetConnected Conference, featuring keynote speakers like former Hewlett-Packard CEO Carly Fiorina, Wharton School Professor Jeremy Siegel, world-renowned rock climber and star of the Academy Award-winning documentary Free Solo Alex Honnold, and NFL Hall of Famer and Superbowl Champion Jerry Rice discussing “Navigating to Next.”

This year’s conference will take place in Keystone, Colorado, from August 18-21. Retirement and wealth management professionals will explore key industry trends and look ahead to what’s next. Representatives from top banks, trust companies, third-party administrators (TPAs), investment advisories, broker-dealers, mutual fund companies, technology providers, and industry media will have the opportunity to learn, network and lead.

Keynote speakers include:

  • Carly Fiorina, former CEO of Hewlett-Packard
  • Jeremy Siegel, Russell E. Palmer Professor of Finance at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania
  • Alex Honnold, world-renowned rock climber and subject of Academy Award-winning documentary Free Solo
  • Jerry Rice, NFL Hall of Famer and three-time Super Bowl champion
  • Carl Benedikt Frey, Program Director and Oxford Martin Citi Fellow, Oxford Martin School, Oxford University

“The rapid evolution in retirement and wealth management is being driven equally by technology and shifting client expectations from a value-add perspective. Professionals are challenged to keep pace by implementing changes to their practice and embracing new technologies while maintaining and growing their client base,” said Cindy Dash, head of Broadridge’s Matrix Financial Solutions. “This year’s GetConnected Conference will provide attendees with tools to navigate these challenges, as well as opportunities to network with peers and potential partners and time to focus on their own careers and business evolution.”

GetConnected will feature keynotes, panel discussions and interactive breakouts on topics like building an effective practice, sales and marketing techniques, and technology’s role in industry evolution. The event will also feature a pre-conference track for advisors seeking valuable continuing education credits.

Additional speakers and panel topics will be announced in the coming weeks. To register for GetConnected 2019 or to learn more, please visit www.broadridge.com/getconnected2019.
