Redefining retail disclosure: Are you ready for Consumer Composite Investments?

The U.K.’s evolving Consumer Composite Investments (CCI) regulation offers more flexibility for asset managers — and greater clarity for investors.

A trusted partner can help you capture opportunities for innovation and navigate evolving regulatory challenges. Discover how to avoid missteps, gain efficiencies, and enhance investor engagement. 

Impacts of the new Consumer Composite Investments regulation

New rules

New rules

The CCI regulation: Replaces the current PRIIPs and UCITS regimes Defines a CCI as an investment whose returns are dependent on the performance of (or changes in the value of) indirect investments Prescribes a new, more flexible template Applies to manufacturers and distributors selling CCIs to U.K. investors

New responsibilities for manufacturers

New responsibilities for manufacturers

Provide distributors with a Product Summary to be given to consumers Give distributors core data in a machine-readable format

New responsibilities for distributors

New responsibilities for distributors

Provide customers with a Product Summary before and after the sale Collaborate with manufacturers to meet investors’ information needs

New opportunities

New opportunities

Improve investor engagement with an easy-to-understand Product Summary Tailor a unique disclosure document for each product Take a more innovative approach to disclosure Enhance digital communications

What will CCI cover?

  • Open-ended funds (excluding pensions)
  • Closed-ended funds
  • Insurance-based investment products
  • Structured products
  • Contracts for difference
  • Derivatives and other complex investments

The evolving CCI timeline

Final Rules

December 08, 2025 Policy Statement and Final rules announced by the Financial Conduct Authority.

Transition starts

April 06, 2026 Legislation commences manufacturers can produce a Product Summary or a KID/KIID.

CCI Deadline

08 June, 2027 Deadline for CCIs to move into the new regime. Firms in scope must comply with the full regime.
