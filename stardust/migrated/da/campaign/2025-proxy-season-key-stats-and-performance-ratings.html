Our steadfast commitment to strengthening participation in corporate governance, advancing technology and ensuring operational excellence has resulted in another record-setting season.

Key Highlights

  • We processed a record 544 billion shares this proxy season.
  • E-delivery of retail positions was at a record high of over 255 million positions.
  • 90% of all communications were digital, as a result of Broadridge’s technologies and “preference” processing for householding, e-delivery, and managed accounts.
  • Of the shares voted through Broadridge, 97% were voted electronically.
  • 1,931 meetings were held online using our virtual shareholder meeting solution.
