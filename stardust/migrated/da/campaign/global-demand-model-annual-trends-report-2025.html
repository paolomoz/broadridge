Navigating growth amid global headwinds

The asset management industry stands at an inflection point. While global professionally managed assets are projected to expand to $132 trillion by 2027, this growth trajectory will be shaped by mounting headwinds. Heightened geopolitical uncertainty, evolving U.S. trade dynamics, and persistent inflationary pressures are reshaping the landscape and raising the imperative for disciplined, data‑driven decision making.

Global growth

Global growth

Global professionally managed assets are set to reach $132T in 2027, however, factors such as geopolitical tension, evolving US tariff policies, and stubborn inflation in a higher for longer rate environment introduce significant caution to growth.

Product dynamics

Product dynamics

Active fixed income is projected to draw in $1.4trn in future 3-year net flows, outstripping the $0.8T to passive fixed income – a theme perpetuated when considering private credit.

APAC growth engine

APAC growth engine

Demographic shifts in APAC, including the expanding middle class in India, drive the regions’ projected organic growth, making APAC the strongest growing region globally over the coming three years.

Insurance tailwinds

Insurance tailwinds

Insurance is poised for continued growth due in part to rising insurance premiums, as well as regional drivers such as the transition of assets from DB schemes to insurer balance sheets through pension buyouts.

Retail market share

Retail market share

Retail investors have steadily been representing a greater portion of global assets, from 44% in 2018, they are expected to represent 50% of the total asset pool in 2027.
