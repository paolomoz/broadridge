Template for no-Figma landing pages

This template uses specific modules and styles that allow you to publish a simpler landing page without providing a Figma in your Web request. This template shows all options available, but you do not need to use them all. If you need to use additional modules or variations not shown on this template, you must open a design ticket to get a Figma mockup for your page. Please note that the official Landing Page Copy Template is required for all landing pages.
