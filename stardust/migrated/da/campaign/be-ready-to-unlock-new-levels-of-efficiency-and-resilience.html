Our thought leadership sessions:

Enabler User Group at Sibos
Sunday, 28th September
13:00 – 14:30		 Sharing best practices with Kai Marzenell (Broadridge) and Magnus Hedenberg (Movitz)
Corporate actions unchained: From manual pain to tokenised gain
Tuesday, 30th September
10:30 – 11:15 | Conference Stage 2		 Horacio Barakat, Head of Capital Markets Digital Innovation will share why corporate actions are complex, often involving multiple intermediaries. But what if these processes could be faster, cheaper, and more transparent? Step in tokenisation. Join us to explore and share all the ways tokenisation, automation and DLT are radically streamlining operations and paving the way for growth, as well as some of the challenges and pitfalls to negotiate along the way.
Meet the experts: Transforming capital markets operations, with AWS
Tuesday, 30th September
16:30 – 17:15 | Conference Stage 5		 The capital markets are rapidly evolving, with firms navigating increasing trading volumes, heightened market volatility, faster settlement times and growing interest in 24-hour trading. The rise of disruptive fintech and demand for new trading protocols is driving the industry to become more data-led, client-centric and automated. Against this backdrop, technology is a vital enabler for firms aiming to adapt and thrive. In this session, hosted by AWS, Roger Burkhardt, CTO – Capital Markets, will share how organisations are transforming market infrastructure to meet these challenges through innovation, agility and resiliency. He will also discuss how firms can prepare to leverage ever-increasing volumes of multi-modal data and capitalise on the opportunities presented by generative AI.
Empowering banks in elevating the corporate payment experience
Wednesday, 1st October
13:30 – 14:00 | Swift Stand Stage (SST)		 Ensuring that payment solutions are efficient and data-rich is not only important for consumers; it is vital for corporates too. In this discussion, Kai Marzenell, Director – Swift Product Management, will join other treasury and cash management experts to explore how banks can continue to enhance the end-user experience and improve corporate payment solutions. They will discuss how, by extending ISO 20022, banks are enabling richer data from the source and supporting the delivery of value-added services directly to corporates.
Corporate actions: Breaking the automation barrier
Wednesday, 1st October
16:00 – 16:45		 Despite being one of the least automated processes in securities, corporate actions remain burdened by manual effort, data inconsistencies, and high costs—challenges that have long been accepted as the industry’s status quo. In this session, Michael McPolin, Managing Director of Market Advocacy and Business Change, will explore how emerging technologies, issuer data integration, and standardisation can unlock new efficiencies. He will examine whether the future lies in improving the current model, rethinking it entirely, or finding a path between the two.

Wednesday, 1st October
16:00 – 17:00 | Booth G026		 Come and meet the Broadridge team over a glass of bubbles.
