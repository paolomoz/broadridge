Why is accurate reporting essential?

Accurate reporting builds investor trust, reduces compliance risk, and streamlines year-end processes for both firms and clients. Harmonized data across asset servicing can drive better, tax-smart decisions.

Does Broadridge provide support for income reclassification in tax reporting?

Why are tax reclaims important, and does Broadridge provide support for this?

Tax reclaims are a critical part of asset servicing , helping clients maximize after‑tax performance and remain compliant across markets by ensuring investors recover excess withholding taxes on cross‑border income, improving investment returns. Broadridge supports efficient and effective tax reclaims through our tax technology, and with our xCeptor partnership.
