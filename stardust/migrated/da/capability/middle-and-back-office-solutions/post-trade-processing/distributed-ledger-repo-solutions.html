What challenges do distributed ledger technology in repo markets address?

Distributed ledger technology in repo markets reduces operational risk, increases transparency, and achieves greater collateral management , mobility, and optimization by providing a shared, immutable record of transactions. As firms evaluate the future of repo, DLR demonstrates the evolving role of distributed ledger technology in capital markets.

How does distributed ledger technology support repo processing?

Distributed ledger technology supports repo processing by enabling real ‑ time updates, synchronized records between counterparties, and improved visibility into collateral and settlement status.

Is Distributed Ledger Repo (DLR) used in live markets?

Yes. Broadridge supports advanced institutional adoption of distributed ledger technology in repo markets. The infrastructure is already processing nearly $8 trillion in monthly transactions.
