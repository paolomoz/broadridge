Asset Servicing FAQs

What is asset servicing?

Asset servicing refers to the operational processes that support the administration of securities, such as corporate actions , class actions , income collection, tax reporting , and proxy management on behalf of investors. As training volumes grow, firms seek to digitize these processes to improve speed, efficiency, and transparency.
