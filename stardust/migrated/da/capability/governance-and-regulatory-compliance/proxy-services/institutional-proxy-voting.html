How does pass-through voting work?

Pass-through voting enables asset managers to give proxy voting rights in portfolio companies directly to investors. With Broadridge’s institutional solution, votes are split proportionally based on each investor’s holdings in the fund. Investors then cast votes for the underlying portfolio companies using the same proxy voting platform and workflow they already use for other investments.
