An Innovative Approach to Retail Proxy Voting Participation: Standing Voting Instructions

Here, Broadridge leaders Chuck Callan, Sr. Vice President of Corporate Affairs, and Ryan Wade, General Manager of Corporate Issuer Solutions, examine ExxonMobil’s Retail Share Voting Program and how it can simplify proxy voting for individual investors.

The discussion includes how the program works, shareholder eligibility, and how investors can opt in while continuing to receive proxy materials and retain full control over their preferences.

The conversation also explores early issuer adoption, growing market interest, and what companies should consider when evaluating this type of solution.
