Scaling high-volume electronic trading with resilient, 24/6 infrastructure

Overview

As part of its rapid expansion across global markets and asset classes, a cloud-native financial infrastructure firm required a high-performance order management platform capable of supporting high-volume, low-latency electronic trading. With growth across U.S. and non-U.S. equities, equity options, and white-label execution services — alongside a 24/6 trading model — the firm needed scalable, resilient infrastructure to power its electronic desk without performance constraints. Broadridge partnered to deliver a low touch OMS platform designed for multi-asset expansion, continuous trading, and seamless global market access.
