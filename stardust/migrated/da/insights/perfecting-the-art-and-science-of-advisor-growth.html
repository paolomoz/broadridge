OVERVIEW

Creating meaningful conversations with marketing

We’ve all seen those generic email newsletters and awkward LinkedIn posts from wealth management firms and advisors attempting to build client engagement, but too often missing the mark with stale news or messages that are too watered-down to connect. Advisors generally understand that sharing timely articles and offering their perspective on breaking financial news, world events, and lifestyle trends can be a great way to stay relevant with clients and prospects. But the details often get in the way. Subscription paywalls, lengthy compliance review processes, and cumbersome user interfaces make it harder than it should be to share great content.

By working with Broadridge, Mandeville Private Client Inc. was able to overcome those challenges, creating a one-stop digital content and social media sharing platform that is helping advisors effortlessly convert today’s big headlines into meaningful engagement opportunities with clients and prospects.

CHALLENGE

Not everyone has time to be an influencer

We live in a world where sharing information and offering hot-takes on the news of the day has become a form of cultural relevance. But, who has the time to read everything, form an interesting perspective, and broadcast it across dozens of different platforms, each with its own different interface and rules of engagement? Especially when they have a business to run!

Staying top-of-mind with clients and prospects in this environment is tough.

On top of these issues, there is the growing risk of saying the wrong thing in an increasingly polarized political climate or even sharing information from unvetted or unreliable sources. Mandeville recognized early on that the future of the wealth management client experience was digital. To ensure advisors remained relevant to current clients and future generations, the firm focused on equipping them with the tools and technology needed to foster meaningful connections in a way that’s both user-friendly and compliant.

SOLUTION

Sharing is caring

Broadridge developed its AdvisorStream solution to solve the digital content marketing challenges facing advisors by delivering a centralized, omni-channel platform that delivers the following:

  • Complete advisor marketing platform: Social media management, email marketing, client newsletters, lead nurturing, lead-optimized pages, website integration, unlimited content, AI-powered insights, smart automation, ecards, robust compliance, and customer support in a single platform.
  • Enterprise-wide integration: Daily updated content, lead-optimized landing pages, and smart automation capabilities are connected throughout enterprise marketing, sales, and compliance workflows, ensuring timely delivery and seamless synchronization across all client and prospect communications.
  • World-class content: Access to one of the industry’s largest libraries of investor focused content through partnerships with leading publishers. The library features licensed materials covering a wide range of topics relevant to investors. Firms can also integrate their own content, with engagement metrics tracked and summarized in detailed reports.

Through a single, easy-to-use interface, AdvisorStream allowed Mandeville’s advisors to quickly identify relevant news and select stories to include in personal newsletters, social media posts and other highly customizable formats to share with all or select clients. In addition, AI-powered analytics built into the solution surface articles of note to specific clients, helping advisors deliver a level of personalized client engagement at scale that was never before possible.

Best of all, AdvisorStream was able to deliver this content in both English and French to address Mandeville’s unique client demographics.

BENEFITS

Engagement  action

This firm-wide, streamlined approach to digital content marketing quickly gained traction among Mandeville’s advisor population. By integrating AdvisorStream, Mandeville has seen widespread adoption with 80% of its advisors actively using it to connect with clients and prospects. AdvisorStream legally licenses high-quality content from leading publishers, like The Wall Street Journal, Barron’s, Globe & Mail and the Financial Times, empowering Mandeville’s advisors to drive client engagement and acquire new clients.

Some of the success Mandeville was able to achieve:

  • 75% of leads generated through social media
  • 100% higher click through rate than the industry average
  • 48,000+ social shares since the 2018 launch
