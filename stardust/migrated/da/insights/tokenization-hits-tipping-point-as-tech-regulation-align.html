Tokenization hits tipping point as tech, regulation align

Tokenization is no longer just a future concept — it’s becoming a market reality.

At the ICI Innovate Conference, Broadridge’s Harsh Choudhary, Head of Product Management, Regulatory Communications, explains why tokenization is gaining real momentum today. In this interview, he discusses how advances in blockchain technology, a rapidly evolving regulatory environment, and growing demand for liquidity and near-instant transactions are turning tokenization into a practical, scalable solution for financial institutions.
