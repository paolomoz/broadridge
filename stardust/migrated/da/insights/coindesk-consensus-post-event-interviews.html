Insights from Consensus 2026

The Conversations Shaping the Future of Digital Assets.

What happens when digital assets enter a new phase as DeFi grows, Wall Street moves further on-chain, and autonomous AI gains momentum? Consensus 2026 had the answers. Attended by more than 20,000 innovators and decision-makers, the event spotlighted the trends transforming tokenization and digital assets. Watch the clips to hear the ideas defining what comes next.

Digital asset adoption starts with familiar experiences

Digital asset adoption in wealth management will depend on delivering familiar experiences for both advisors and investors. Broadridge Chief Digital Officer Rob Krugman explores how firms are integrating digital assets into existing platforms, workflows, and client relationships.

Bridging traditional and digital markets

As institutions explore digital assets, many are looking for ways to integrate them into existing workflows and operating models. Germán Soto Sanchez, Broadridge Chief Product Officer and Co-President Digital Assets, shares how firms can bridge traditional and digital markets through integrated infrastructure and operating models.

The infrastructure behind integration

As firms expand access to digital assets, they must also integrate the workflows, data, and processes that support them. Explore the operational infrastructure required to connect on-chain and traditional assets across reporting, tax, and servicing functions.

Unlocking liquidity through tokenization

The promise of tokenization lies in increasing the velocity of assets as they move through the financial system. Horacio Barakat, Broadridge Global Head of Digital Innovation, discusses how tokenization can help unlock greater liquidity, mobility, and efficiency for traditional financial assets.

Tokenized collateral in action

Tokenized collateral is creating new opportunities to improve liquidity and funding efficiency. Find out how tokenized repo markets are helping firms access capital more quickly while reducing funding costs.

Governance for the next phase of growth

As tokenized markets mature, governance will become increasingly important. Discover how platforms are adapting to support multiple forms of tokenized assets.
