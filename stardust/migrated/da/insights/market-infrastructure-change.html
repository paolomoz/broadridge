Market Infrastructure Change

New research on where capital markets firms are prioritising  post-trade and investment operations spend.

Download the report

Mandatory change, finite budgets and rising expectations are forcing firms to make sharper technology investment choices in 2026. From legacy platform modernisation and market infrastructure transformation to T+1, US Treasury Central Clearing, 24/5 trading and AI, firms are balancing urgent delivery with long-term competitiveness.

Our global survey explores where firms are spending, what is driving change across the trade cycle, and where leaders see true operational differentiation. The findings show an industry under pressure to modernise, contain costs, respond to market change and free up budget for innovation.

What leaders should do next

The research suggests five priorities for this year:

  • Prioritise mandatory market and regulatory change without losing strategic focus
  • Reduce legacy drag and create room for innovation
  • Align budgets to capabilities that truly differentiate the franchise
  • Adopt a pragmatic build-buy-partner approach
  • Strengthen governance across vendors, delivery partners and change portfolios
