Navigating the Transition to EDGAR Next — A Comprehensive Guide for Asset Managers

Our guidebook explains the SEC’s shift to a more secure, Login.gov–based filing system, replacing shared credentials with individualized access. Asset managers must prepare by updating credentials, designating administrators, and enrolling by December 19, 2025 to avoid filing disruptions.

The guide outlines how to manage roles, delegate filing authority to Broadridge, and adapt compliance processes. We support asset managers with enrollment services, ongoing account administration, and technical assistance for a smooth and secure transition to EDGAR Next. Download the guide.

Contested Proxy Votes for Closed-End Funds: Playing to Win

Our article highlights the surge in activist investors targeting closed-end funds, with a few firms driving most of the contested proxy activity. As these battles increase, asset managers must treat contested votes like political campaigns, requiring real-time voting data, persuasive messaging, and strong communication strategies.

Success depends on choosing the right proxy service provider to guarantee vote integrity and a skilled solicitor to run an effective campaign. While management usually has the advantage, missteps can be costly, so preparation, partner selection, and disciplined execution are critical to winning contested proxy votes. Read the article.
