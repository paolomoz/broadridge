Private Credit Market Growth

Is your firm prepared for the future?

In the rapidly evolving private debt industry, asset managers require robust tools to navigate complexity and drive success. This checklist outlines essential features of a private debt portfolio and order management system, including integrated borrowing base calculations, covenant compliance monitoring, efficient portfolio tracking, and customized reporting capabilities. With scalability, transparency, and real-time compliance at the forefront, these tools empower asset managers to meet growing investor demands, regulatory scrutiny, and operational challenges in a dynamic market.

Checklist Key Considerations
Evaluate the system’s ability to track borrowing base calculations with up-to-date collateral valuations and ensure compliance with loan covenants.
Review the ability to implement effective governance and contingency planning through regular monitoring and transparent reporting.
Understand the effect of potential investments quickly and accurately with robust scenario analysis tailored for private credit.
Assess whether the system can meet flexible and tailored reporting requirements, providing insights that are crucial for decision-makers in the private credit arena.
Streamline and automate the creation and distribution of invoices, statements, and notices to ensure efficiency, accuracy, and compliance.
Determine if the software can accommodate the expansion of your private credit portfolios and manage a growing number of investments.
Examine how the system meets investors’ increasing demands for transparency and heightens scrutiny from regulators and rating agencies, with real-time updates.
