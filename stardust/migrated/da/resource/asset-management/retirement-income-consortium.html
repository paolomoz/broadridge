Retirement Income Consortium

Empowering the adoption of guaranteed income options within retirement plans.

Educating. Evaluating. Evangelizing.

Expanded access to lifetime income within employer-sponsored retirement plans is crucial. In an era where fewer workers have access to a pension along with the declining reliability of social security, retirees are relying heavily on their workplace savings for retirement income.

That’s why the Retirement Income Consortium is educating the industry on the need, created a formalized due-diligence process for evaluating offerings, and are working together to evangelize for adoption.
