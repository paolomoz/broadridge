GenAI-Powered Analytics Feature for Post-Trade Processing

Post-trade reporting can be overwhelming, particularly with evolving regulatory demands and increasing operational risks. Broadridge's GenAI-powered analytics feature on the multi-asset post-trade processing platform, part of Broadridge's OpsGPT® application, addresses these challenges by streamlining operations and enhancing risk management.

GenAI-Powered Analytics Feature for Post-Trade Processing

Video Transcript

Post-trade reporting can be overwhelming, particularly with evolving regulatory demands and increasing operational risks. This complexity often makes it challenging to extract valuable insights from large datasets using traditional reporting tools.

We have now enhanced our industry-leading post-trade platform with the innovative GenAI-powered analytics feature. This tool enables users to generate insights and visualizations directly from their trade data using natural language.

Integrated seamlessly into our multi-asset post-trade processing platform, this advanced analytics feature is part of Broadridge’s OpsGPT® application, leveraging existing user data within the system.

There’s no need for data migration – it’s fully operational for our post-trade platform users from the start. With this feature, you can effortlessly manage day-to-day tasks, generate visualizations, and pose predictive questions tailored to your needs, improving risk management and operational efficiency.

Simply by clicking the tool on our post-trade platform, you can “chat” with any generated report and quickly access actionable insights. Intuitive AI-driven questions empower users to dive deeper into their data and enhance your reporting workflow simply with natural language. Interactive visualizations and follow-up questions are just a click away, streamlining your reporting process.

Built from day one to support multiple languages and assist regional users, it provides multiple language options, including English, Japanese and Chinese

Engineered for risk management and efficiency, it helps firms scale operations amid regulatory changes, minimize operational risks, and reduce reliance on manual reporting.

The seamless technical integration allows your post-trade data to flow smoothly into generative AI capabilities. From automatically detecting data schemas and generating predictive questions to rendering interactive visualizations, your data can be transformed into valuable insights instantly.

Built on the latest technology architecture, we ensure data security and minimize disruptions while harnessing the power of AI capabilities.

Transform your reporting and manage risk effectively with GenAI-powered advanced analytics today.

Key Features

  • Natural Language Interactions: Leverages generative AI and natural language interactions to supply instant reports, data visualization, and quickly generated summaries in an intuitive and user-friendly interface.
  • Seamless Integration: An out-of-the-box feature readily available on the post-trade processing platform. It utilizes existing user data on the platform, ensuring smooth functionality, accuracy, and data security without the need for data migration.
  • Multilingual Support: Supports multiple languages, including English, Japanese, and Chinese, making it ideal for international users.
  • Data Visualization & Predictive Follow-Up Questions: Visualize data with just one click and engage in predictive follow-up questions to gain deeper insights.
  • Enhanced Risk Management and Operational Efficiency: Reduces reliance on manual reporting, simplifying business management, which helps firms address growing regulatory demands.
