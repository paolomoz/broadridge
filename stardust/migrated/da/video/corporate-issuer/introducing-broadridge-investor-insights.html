Introducing Broadridge Investor Insights

Powering investor engagement for best-in-class governance.

Video Transcript

Introducing Broadridge Investor Insights - the cutting-edge platform for corporate issuers that revolutionizes shareholder and voting data analysis. This analytics dashboard provides near real-time insights and comprehensive coverage for the majority of eligible shares, offering a deep dive into ownership distribution, investor behaviour, and voting trends.

Access these features in a user-friendly interface, simplifying communication efforts and reducing IR costs.

The platform’s meeting overview section delivers critical information on data coverage, resolution acceptance rates, and countdown to the cut-off date.

Explore individual proposals, identify dissenting shareholders, and predict outcomes at beneficial owner level.

Monitor meeting progress with insights into the custodian chain for each beneficial owner vote.

All data is readily exportable into user-friendly automated reports.

Analyze the geographical breakdown of shareholders and target regions for engagement, while also understanding shareholder categories participating in meetings.

Forecast future voting patterns with AI and machine learning, providing invaluable insights to gauge shareholder sentiment and predict meeting outcomes.

Broadridge Investor Insights - revolutionizing shareholder data analysis for smarter decision-making.
