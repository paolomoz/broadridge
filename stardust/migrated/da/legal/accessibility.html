Accessibility at Broadridge

Accessibility Statement for Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc.

Measures to support accessibility

Broadridge follows the principles below in designing, developing, and operating its consumer facing websites so they may be fully and equally used and enjoyed by people with disabilities as per the Americans with Disabilities Act:

Provide continual accessibility training for our design and development staff.

Assign clear accessibility targets and responsibilities to our design and development teams.

Conformance status


The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) defines requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. It defines three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA. At Broadridge, we have adopted the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as our web accessibility standard for consumer facing websites and we endeavor for these websites to substantially conform to that standard. For more insight about web accessibility, visit the W3C® Web Accessibility Initiative website.


ADA Accessibility Inquiries

We value your feedback regarding the accessibility of our website.
If you encounter any accessibility barriers on this website, please email us a detailed note about the issue at the address below, and we will make every effort to address and remove the obstacle(s).
