Broadridge Appoints Richard Terblanche as Vice President, Global Distribution Data Solutions

LONDON and NEW YORK, May 20, 2026 — Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc. (NYSE: BR) has appointed Richard Terblanche as Vice President, Global Distribution Data Solutions within its growing Data-Driven Fund Solutions business.

Based in London, Richard will focus on working with global asset managers to navigate the increasing complexity of client and distribution data across global markets. This newly created role reflects Broadridge’s ongoing investment in supporting clients as they look to optimize distribution and drive growth.

“We see a transformative opportunity for asset managers to unify and operationalize their global client and distribution data ecosystem. By creating a connected, AI-ready data foundation, firms can accelerate decision-making, streamline operational complexity, and drive growth through AI-powered intelligence and optimization,” said Nigel Birch, Head of Global Data & Analytics. “We’re pleased to welcome Richard to our leadership team and leverage the deep expertise and innovation mindset he brings to help drive this next phase of transformation.”

“I’m excited to join Broadridge at a time when many firms are rethinking how they manage and use data to support decision making,” said Richard Terblanche, VP Global Distribution Data Solutions. “There is a clear opportunity to bring greater clarity and consistency to how this data is understood and applied across the business.”

Richard brings over 20 years of experience across investment management and data. He previously held senior roles at Strategic Insight and most recently led the EMEA business at Fishtank, where he worked with asset managers on complex data and distribution challenges.
