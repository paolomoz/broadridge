Broadridge Appoints Dan Mullooly as Vice President of Product Management – Insurance Segment for Broadridge Customer Communications

NEW YORK, May 14, 2026 — Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc. (NYSE: BR) today announced that Dan Mullooly has joined the company as Vice President, Product Management – Insurance Segment, within Broadridge Customer Communications (BRCC). In this new role, Mullooly will help advance Broadridge’s strategy for modernizing insurance communications and report to Ross Solomon, Head of Product Management for BRCC.

“Dan’s expertise will help us deliver modern solutions for our clients and accelerate growth in our insurance communications business,” said Ross Solomon, Head of Product Management for Broadridge's Customer Communications. “He will play an important role in advancing our strategy and strengthening Broadridge’s position as a trusted partner to the insurance industry.”

Mullooly will lead the strategic roadmap for Broadridge’s Insurance Segment, helping carriers modernize legacy operations with digital-first, omnichannel SaaS solutions. He will partner across the organization to translate customer needs into scalable platform capabilities that drive growth, efficiency, and cost reduction for insurance clients.

“I’m excited to leverage my experience in building data-driven products and advancing digital transformation in the insurance industry,” said Mullooly. “I look forward to helping Broadridge continue modernizing the way we serve insurance clients and address evolving market demands with innovative solutions.”

Mullooly will also play a key role in helping insurance carriers address rising consumer expectations for seamless, omnichannel communications. His leadership will support Broadridge’s efforts to modernize the policyholder communications lifecycle through automated solutions that strengthen Broadridge’s role as a strategic partner in the insurance industry’s ongoing transformation.

With more than 14 years of experience spanning insurance and fintech, Mullooly brings a strong track record of leading digital transformation, scaling enterprise platforms, and delivering data-driven product innovation. His data-driven approach and ability to translate market needs into impactful product strategies make him well suited for this role.
