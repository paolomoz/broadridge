WhiteHawk Capital Partners Scales Direct Lending with Broadridge

WhiteHawk implements Broadridge’s investment management platform to drive growth, support complex loan structures and provide operational scale

NEW YORK, June 1, 2026 — Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc. (NYSE: BR), a global Fintech leader, today announced that WhiteHawk Capital Partners, LP is scaling its direct lending platform with the implementation of Broadridge’s Sentry investment management solution. By modernizing and centralizing its middle- and back-office operations on an institutional-grade platform, WhiteHawk is streamlining loan administration, strengthening data integrity, and enhancing transparency for co-investors — building the operational foundation needed to support larger, more complex private debt strategies as the firm continues to grow.

“Private credit managers can’t scale tomorrow’s strategies on yesterday’s infrastructure,” said Mike Sleightholme, Head of Asset Management and President of Broadridge International. “We’re empowering WhiteHawk with purpose-built technology that cuts through operational complexity, enhances transparency and gives them the confidence and control to grow larger, longer-dated private investments at scale.”

The Sentry platform supports WhiteHawk’s ability to scale loan size and duration across its funds while maintaining strong operational control and data integrity. Its flexibility in supporting complex loan structures was a key factor in the firm’s selection, aligning with the demands of an expanding direct lending strategy.

“Adopting an institutional-grade best in class solution gives us a scalable foundation for managing our private debt portfolios,” said Harry Chung, Chief Operating Officer and Chief Financial Officer at WhiteHawk Capital Partners. “The direct lending and agent functionality allows our teams to efficiently administer loans and support continued growth.”

WhiteHawk’s adoption reflects a broader shift across the private credit market, as managers move away from manual processes toward platforms designed to scale, support regulatory needs, and provide investor transparency.

Broadridge delivers a unified investment management platform that automates critical processes across the front, middle and back offices – connecting trading, portfolio management, risk, operations and compliance within a single framework. By centralizing data and workflows across the lifecycle, Sentry reduces operational complexity, eliminates the need to build and maintain multiple systems or scale operational teams, and enables firms to focus on generating alpha and driving growth.
