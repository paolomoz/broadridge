Broadridge and Pirum have Agreed to Work Together on Connecting Broadridge’s SFCM platform with Pirum TradeConnect

The collaboration reflects a shared commitment to improving connectivity across the securities finance ecosystem and supporting clients with more efficient front-to-back workflows. By aligning Broadridge’s trading and connectivity capabilities with Pirum’s post-trade and new pre-trade infrastructure, the firms aim to help support market participants as they navigate evolving operational demands, including T+1 readiness, broader market access, and the need for greater efficiency across the transaction lifecycle.

The agreement also supports both firms’ ambitions to help clients access new markets and broader pools of global liquidity, while strengthening the links between trading activity and downstream post-trade processing.

Comment from Rob Frost, Pirum

Rob Frost, Global Head of Business Solutions at Pirum, said:

“We are pleased to be working with Broadridge on this initiative. Connecting Pirum TradeConnect with Broadridge’s SFCM platform is a positive step in expanding industry connectivity and helping clients benefit from more streamlined and efficient workflows. As market participants look for greater automation, better access to liquidity, and stronger front-to-back integration, this collaboration reflects our shared focus on delivering practical solutions that support the evolving needs of the global securities finance market.”

Comment from Tina Joshi, Broadridge

Tina Joshi, Head of Securities Finance Product Solutions at Broadridge, said:

We are delighted to be collaborating with Pirum on this agreement. Broadridge remains focused on building scalable, connected solutions that help clients respond to market structure change and increasing operational complexity. Working with Pirum supports our broader strategy of enhancing interoperability across the securities finance lifecycle and helping clients access more efficient workflows, stronger post-trade alignment, and opportunities in both established and developing markets.
