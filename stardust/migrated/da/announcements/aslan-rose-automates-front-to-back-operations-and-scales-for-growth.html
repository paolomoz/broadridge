Aslan Rose Automates Front-to-Back Operations and Scales for Growth with Broadridge Investment Management Solutions

NEW YORK, April 9, 2026 - Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc. (NYSE:BR) today announced that alternative asset manager Aslan Rose, LLC has gone live on its integrated front-to-back investment management technology and operations platform. The implementation positions Aslan Rose with a scalable operating model designed to support its next phase of growth, enhance efficiency, and allow its team to remain focused on investment performance and client outcomes.

“As an emerging manager, it was important to me to put a solid and capable operational foundation in place from the start,” said Ertan Yenicay, Founder of Aslan Rose. “Broadridge has been a great partner, allowing me to confidently stay focused on managing capital and growing the firm.”

Aslan Rose is using a comprehensive investment management solution suite that includes Broadridge’s portfolio management, order management, accounting, risk, and managed services, bringing front-office, middle-office, and back-office technology and operations together within a single, consolidated environment. As part of the implementation, Aslan Rose is also an early adopter of Broadridge’s newly launched general ledger accounting capabilities, strengthening its accounting and reporting infrastructure while supporting greater automation across the investment lifecycle.

“Emerging managers increasingly want a single, scalable operating model that can grow with them,” said Mike Sleightholme, President of Asset Management Solutions at Broadridge. “Aslan Rose’s implementation demonstrates how firms can establish a strong front-to-back foundation while keeping their teams focused on investment outcomes and AUM growth.”

Broadridge delivers a unified investment management platform that automates critical processes across the front, middle and back offices – connecting trading, portfolio management, risk, operations and compliance within a single framework. By centralizing data and workflows across the lifecycle, Broadridge reduces operational complexity, eliminates the need to build and maintain multiple systems or scale operational teams, and enables firms to focus on generating alpha and driving growth.
