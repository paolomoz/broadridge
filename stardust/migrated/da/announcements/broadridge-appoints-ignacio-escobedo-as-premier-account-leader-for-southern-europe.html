Broadridge Appoints Ignacio Escobedo as Premier Account Leader for Southern Europe

Seasoned financial services executive to deepen strategic client relationships and increase growth across key Southern European accounts

Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc. (NYSE: BR) today announced that Ignacio Escobedo has joined the company as Premier Account Leader (PAL) for Southern Europe. In this role, Escobedo will focus on strengthening enterprise-wide client relationships and accelerating growth across some of Broadridge’s most strategic accounts in the region.

“As Broadridge continues to expand coverage of key accounts across Europe and Asia, the Premier Account Leader role is central to how we deepen strategic engagement with clients,” said Mark Gidley, Head of EMEA & APAC Account and Commercial Management at Broadridge. “Ignacio brings deep international experience, a proven growth mindset, and a strong ability to build trusted executive relationships across markets. We are very pleased to welcome him to Broadridge.”

Escobedo joins Broadridge from the London Stock Exchange Group, bringing more than 27 years of international experience in financial services, data strategy and business development. He has successfully led global strategic accounts, driven business growth, and built senior client relationships across multiple markets, business lines, and client divisions.

“Broadridge is a trusted partner to the financial services industry, and I am excited to join the firm at a time of strong momentum opportunity,” said Ignacio Escobedo. “I look forward to working with clients and colleagues to strengthen strategic partnerships and help advance their business priorities across Southern Europe and beyond.”
