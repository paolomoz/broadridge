Customer Security Controls Framework

Independent assessment for Swift users

The Customer Security Programme (CSP) requires Swift users to assess the level of implementation of their security standards and report back to Swift.

In 2021, Swift announced that all companies connected to the Swift network must have the security of their infrastructure independently audited on an annual basis.

Broadridge has successfully completed over 100 assessments. As a Swift specialist with 25 years managing a Swift Service Bureau, we combine two fundamentally important qualities:

  • We have a deep understanding of the Swift infrastructure. Our assessment service is timely and cost-effective.
  • We work with qualified independent auditors to complete the service so that independence from our original Swift business service is assured.
