Proxy voting, reimagined

The ProxyVote app allows investors to access, review and vote all their proxies as well as confirm their vote was counted in only a few swipes. Download it today and experience the convenience and simplicity investors expect from a mobile app.

  • Manage, review and vote in only a few taps
  • Create a unique profile and never need a control number again
  • Pre-set voting instructions for eligible meetings
  • Get alerts about upcoming meetings based on topics of interest
  • Access links to information about the Directors up for election and the individual meeting proposals
  • Receive final meeting results once meeting is complete
