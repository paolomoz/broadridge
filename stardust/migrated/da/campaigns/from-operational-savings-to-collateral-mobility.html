The Next Phase of Tokenization in APAC

Download the Report

Based on insights from an industry event in Singapore hosted with Value Exchange and featuring leaders from ABN AMRO, HSBC, and Broadridge, this report explores how firms are moving beyond tokenization pilots to practical implementation.

Discover how tokenized collateral and collateral immobilization models are enabling 24/7 collateral mobility, supporting cross-currency repo, and helping institutions manage liquidity risk across time zones, without replacing existing infrastructure.

Access the full report to learn:

  • How tokenization can address the liquidity gap between US and Asian markets
  • Why DLT is emerging as a powerful tool for liquidity risk management
  • How cross-currency collateral mobility can expand funding access in APAC
  • What practical adoption looks like alongside existing systems
