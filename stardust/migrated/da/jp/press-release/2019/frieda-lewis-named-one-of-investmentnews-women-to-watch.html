Frieda Lewis Named One of InvestmentNews’ Women to Watch

auto

NEW YORK, November 25, 2019 – Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc. (NYSE:BR), a global Fintech leader and part of the S&P 500® Index, is proud to highlight that Frieda Lewis has been recognized as one of InvestmentNews’ 2019 Women to Watch. As one of the 20 prestigious winners, Frieda was chosen from several hundred nominations for her leadership, contributions and impact in the financial advice industry.

“Frieda has been a trailblazer throughout her career and represents the best of Broadridge and the financial services industry,” said Chris Perry, head of global sales, marketing and client solutions, Broadridge. “Her passion and commitment drive positive outcomes not just for clients but also are a model for colleagues and business partners. Frieda fosters a culture of inclusion and inspires people to do their best work.”

Each of the advisers and executives who made the fifth annual InvestmentNews Women to Watch list were chosen by a rigorous selection process designed to identify women who possess leadership skills, the ability to effect change in the industry, a willingness to share their expertise with other women, and are committed to giving back to the community.

“It is an honor to be included in this list of distinguished business leaders,” said Frieda Lewis, Broadridge’s chief commercial diversity officer. “My job is to cultivate commercial opportunities across Broadridge and serve as a strategic counselor to our clients. This takes shape in many ways – including discussing the positive impact that diversity has on business outcomes and advising clients on D&I (Diversity & Inclusion) best practices.”

Frieda will receive her award at the fifth annual InvestmentNews Women to Watch Luncheon on March 12, 2020, at the Ziegfeld Ballroom in New York City.

To learn more about Frieda, the 2019 winners, or the awards and luncheon, please visit Investmentnews.com/2019women.
