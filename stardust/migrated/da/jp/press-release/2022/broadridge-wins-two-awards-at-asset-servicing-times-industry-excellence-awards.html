Broadridge Wins Two Awards at This Year’s Asset Servicing Times Industry Excellence Awards

LONDON – November 14, 2022 – Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc. (NYSE:BR), a global Fintech leader, has won the Best Corporate Actions Technology and Best Asset Servicing Technology Product awards at this year’s Asset Servicing Times Industry Excellence Awards, an industry-renowned ceremony recognizing the most influential names and businesses in the financial services market. These awards recognize Broadridge’s commitment to transforming proxy voting and the entire asset servicing lifecycle through next-generation technology.

“We’re honored to recognize Broadridge at this year’s Asset Servicing Times Industry Excellence Awards for their continued efforts and success in transforming proxy voting and the management of corporate actions,” said Justin Lawson, Co-Founder and Publisher at Asset Servicing Times. “Every year, we’re looking to spotlight the most talented and dedicated firms and individuals that are exhibiting best practices across our industry, and Broadridge is doing just that.”.

Best Corporate Actions Technology Award

Broadridge’s automated end-to-end Global Asset Servicing Solution streamlines corporate actions processing, from announcement data capture and cleansing to client election management and payment processing. Broadridge’s clients benefit from a single global solution, improving operational efficiency, timeliness and accuracy of processing, whilst reducing the costly risk associated with missing corporate actions. Broadridge remains at the forefront of delivering Cloud hosted technology-driven solutions that power corporate actions automation, and drive opportunities for the front-office.

Best Asset Servicing Technology Product Award

Broadridge continues to evolve and innovate its Global Proxy solution suite to advance corporate governance standards globally, as a result of substantial annual investments in both retail and institutional voting technology and through extended local market digital connectivity. These investments have expanded Broadridge’s local market “golden copy” event sourcing and vote execution capabilities, extended voting windows for investors and lowered overall risk to users, while enhancing end-to-end shareholder engagement and democracy.

“We are delighted to be recognized for our dedication to global proxy services transformation and the automation of corporate actions,” said Samir Pandiri, President of Broadridge International. “At Broadridge, we’re continuing to develop and advance our solution, driving innovation and continually raising the bar leveraging digital technology solutions that propel the market forward to meet the growing needs of our clients and the industry. Congratulations also to all of the other winners that were honored at the ceremony.”
