Elite Athlete Management Program

Elite athletes balance work and training through our unique program

In 2022, Broadridge officially partnered with USRowing to support and empower the US Women’s National Team. At the heart of our partnership with USRowing is our creation of the Elite Athlete Management Program, an initiative designed to provide support to USRowing Olympic hopefuls and athletes. Through part-time employment positions, athletes not only gain essential work experience, but also a flexible work environment and crucial economic support during their training regimens for world championships, Olympics, and Paralympic competitions.

At Broadridge, we recognize that success is not just achieved through individual prowess but through the collective strength of a unified team. In rowing, as in our business, feeling connected toward a mission – sometimes greater than yourself – is the cornerstone of achievement. That is how every member of the crew propels the boat forward, and how we at Broadridge empower our people to advance industry change.
