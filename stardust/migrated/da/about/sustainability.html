Driving Sustainability to Make a Difference for Our Associates, Clients, Investors, and Communities

auto

Corporate sustainability is the foundation of how we operate our company.

Creating long-term value for our clients requires a deep commitment to sustainability. We believe there is a direct connection between employee engagement, client satisfaction, and the creation of shareholder value – a concept known as the Service-Profit Chain. At Broadridge, we put this idea to work not only in our internal policies and processes but also in our communities, just as we do for our valued partners. We recognize that the stronger our commitments to sustainable practices are internally and in the communities around us, the more value we can bring to the marketplace.

“At Broadridge, we have always believed in doing well by doing good. We are committed to supporting our associates, clients, investors, and the communities where we live and work to create the best long-term outcomes for all.”

Environmental

We have committed to addressing our environmental impact through a three-pronged approach: supporting our clients’ environmental goals through our print-to-digital strategy; leveraging alternative energy sources within our operations and pursuing responsible procurement and disposal practices within our supply chain.

People and Communities

At Broadridge, our associates are our greatest asset. Our goal is to attract, develop, and retain the most talented individuals in the industry. We are focused on creating an environment where every associate can bring their authentic self to a workplace that fosters engagement, a sense of belonging, and an emphasis on delivering exceptional client service. We strive to ensure that our associates represent the clients we serve and the communities where we live and work. We put our core values into action to effect lasting change through the Broadridge Foundation.

Policies and Practices

We believe strong corporate governance practices and policies are integral in fostering a culture of integrity, managing a better-performing and sustainable business, and achieving long-term shareholder value. Strong corporate governance starts with an independent and experienced Board of Directors. Through our framework of policies, processes, and Board oversight, we focus on better managing our business and aligning the interests of management with those of our stakeholders.

We also support strong corporate governance by providing critical governance infrastructure and communications solutions.
