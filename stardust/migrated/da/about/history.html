Our History

We first opened our doors in 1962 as the newly formed ADP Brokerage Services Group with one client processing an average of 300 trades per night.

Since then, we’ve grown into a thriving, multi-billion-dollar global technology leader serving clients across the world. We handle millions of trades each day involving trillions of dollars, support over 7 billion communications a year, and manage shareholder voting in 120 countries.

Throughout this evolution, our mission has always stayed the same – to empower confident decision making and get our clients ready for what’s next.
